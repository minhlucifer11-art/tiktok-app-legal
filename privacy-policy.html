Privacy Policy

Last updated:

n8n Video Publisher (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an internal automation tool that helps authorized users publish video content to their own TikTok accounts using TikTok’s official APIs.

Information We Collect

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access/refresh tokens) provided after you authorize the app.
  • Basic publishing metadata you provide (e.g., captions, video URLs) to perform posting actions.

How We Use Information

  • To upload and publish videos to your TikTok account after your explicit authorization.
  • To manage posting workflows and troubleshoot operational issues (e.g., failed uploads).

Media Hosting

Video files are hosted and delivered via Cloudinary. Cloudinary is used solely for media storage and delivery.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your TikTok OAuth tokens with third parties, except as required to operate the service (e.g., API requests to TikTok and media delivery via Cloudinary).

Data Security & Retention

We store tokens and configuration data only as long as necessary to provide the publishing service. We use reasonable measures to protect stored data against unauthorized access.

Your Choices

  • You may revoke the app’s access from your TikTok account settings at any time.
  • You may request deletion of stored tokens and related data by contacting us.
